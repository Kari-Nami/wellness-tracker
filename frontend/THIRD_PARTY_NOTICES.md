# Font licenses

Daywell bundles the Manrope and Fraunces typefaces locally. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their license notices are included in `public/licenses/manrope-OFL.txt` and `public/licenses/fraunces-OFL.txt`, and ship with the static application.

The Daywell brand mark is an original vector asset in `public/brand-mark.svg`.
